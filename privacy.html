Privacy Policy for Prismo Calendar

Last updated: October 2026

Purpose

Prismo Calendar is a private personal scheduling tool. It accesses Google Calendar data only at the direction of its authorized user.

Use of Google user data

Google Calendar information is used solely to read, create, update, and organize the authorized user's calendar events.

Data sharing

Prismo Calendar does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties.

Data storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored privately on the user's self-hosted system. Credentials and calendar information are not published on this website.

Revoking access

The user may revoke access at any time through the security settings of their Google Account.

Data deletion and contact

To request deletion of locally stored authorization data, contact: privacy.blasphemy677@slmails.com
